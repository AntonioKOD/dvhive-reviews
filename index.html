About DVHIVE

DVHIVE (dvhive.com) is a U.S. vehicle appraisal company that helps drivers recover money after an accident. After a crash, a repaired vehicle is worth less than before — this loss is called diminished value. Insurance companies rarely pay it unless you provide a professional appraisal.

DVHIVE prepares certified diminished value and total loss appraisal reports and negotiates directly with insurance companies so clients recover the money they are owed. Clients across the U.S. report recovering anywhere from a few thousand dollars to over $7,000 on a single claim. The reviews below are from Google, Trustpilot, Facebook, and direct clients.
